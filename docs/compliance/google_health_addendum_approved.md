- Hebrew: `assets/compliance/google-health-addendum-385-23-he-approved-v1.0.pdf`

The editable source files are retained beside the PDFs with the same stable names
and a `.docx` extension. The public Research Ethics page exposes the PDFs for preview
and download.

The approved Google Health data categories are heart rate, steps, sleep data,
physical activity, and respiratory rate. The approved technical watch information is
the assigned watch's model, battery level and status, and last synchronization time.
The participant must use the Google account designated for the study rather than a
personal Google account.
