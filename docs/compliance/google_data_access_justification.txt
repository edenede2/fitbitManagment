AdmonTracker supports University of Haifa ethics-approved Study 385/23, which examines relationships among childhood experiences, sleep patterns, and emotional, behavioral, and cognitive responses in adulthood. We request read-only Google Health access to: (1) activity_and_fitness for steps and physical activity; (2) health_metrics_and_measurements for heart rate and respiratory rate; and (3) sleep for sleep sessions, duration, stages, and timestamps. These data are used only for the study and necessary data-quality/completeness checks. The assigned watch's model, battery level/status, and last synchronization time are collected separately for charging and synchronization support; they do not justify an additional Google Health scope. More limited scopes cannot provide all five measurements named in the approved participant addendum. We request no write or mindfulness access and do not use the data for advertising, medical decisions, or unrelated AI training.
