AdmonTracker supports University of Haifa ethics-approved Study 385/23 on relationships among childhood experiences, sleep patterns, and emotional, behavioral, and cognitive responses in adulthood. We request read-only Google Health access to: (1) activity_and_fitness for steps and physical activity; (2) health_metrics_and_measurements for heart rate and respiratory rate; (3) sleep for sleep sessions, duration, stages, and timestamps; and (4) settings only for the assigned watch's model, battery level/status, and last synchronization time. Staff use the device fields to identify charging or synchronization problems and assess data completeness; the app does not modify settings. These scopes are the narrowest permissions covering the data named in the approved participant addendum. We request no write or mindfulness access and do not use data for advertising, medical decisions, or unrelated AI training.
